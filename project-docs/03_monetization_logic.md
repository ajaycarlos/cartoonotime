# Monetization & "Transformative Use" Logic

This document details the strategy to ensure the YouTube Shorts generated by this pipeline comply with YouTube's monetization policies, specifically avoiding the "Reused Content" penalty.

## The YouTube Policy Context
YouTube strictly prohibits channels from simply re-uploading other people's content without significant original commentary or educational value. Channels that upload unedited cartoon clips are frequently demonetized or rejected from the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) under the "Reused Content" policy.

## The Strategy: Transformative Educational Value
To convert standard cartoon clips into highly valuable, monetizable content, our pipeline employs a **"Transformative Use"** strategy through the automated injection of educational trivia.

### 1. Wikipedia Integration
- The script (`caption_trivia.py`) automatically queries the Wikipedia API using the cartoon's title or main subject.
- It extracts the first two sentences of the Wikipedia article, providing factual, educational context.

### 2. The "Fun Fact" Overlay
- The extracted text is dynamically summarized into a digestible "Fun Fact".
- This fact is visually burned into the video as a large, readable overlay on the bottom 30% of the screen.
- A semi-transparent black background is placed behind the text to ensure accessibility and readability.

### Why This Works for Monetization
- **Added Educational Value:** The video is no longer just an entertainment clip; it is an educational Short that teaches the viewer something new about the cartoon's history, production, or cultural impact.
- **Algorithm & Policy Compliance:** By substantively altering the original work with factual commentary, the final output meets the criteria for "Transformative Content". The viewer's experience is fundamentally changed, thereby establishing original value required for YouTube monetization approval.
- **Engagement:** Text overlays in the lower third of vertical videos have been shown to increase watch time, as users spend time reading the fact while the visual loops, signaling high engagement to the Shorts algorithm.
